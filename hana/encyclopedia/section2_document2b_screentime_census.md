## 3. 🔥🔥 THE FINDING THAT COST ME TWO ERRATA AND IS WORTH MORE THAN THE CENSUS

**The single most-voted chapter in the franchise — ch. 59, 6,854 ballots — is not Nino's. I have been telling you for two documents that it was. It's ICHIKA'S.**
The Kodansha TOC for Vol.7 reads, in order: 「第55話 **最後の試験が三玖の場合**」/「第56話 …**四葉**の場合」/「第57話 …**五月**の場合」/「第58話」. **Vol.7 ends at 59, and Vol.8 begins at 60「攻略開始」 — so 59 is the second half of 「最後の祭りが… 一花の場合①②」-pattern branch: 58 = 二乃の場合 (Nino, the one-chapter branch), 59–60 = 一花の場合①② (Ichika, the two-chapter branch).**
**Corrected mapping, effective immediately in all four files:** `55=Miku · 56=Yotsuba · 57=Itsuki · 58=Nino · 59–60=Ichika`.

> ### 🤓 WHY THE CORRECTION MATTERS MORE THAN THE ERROR
> **I built a thesis on "a non-winning sister's branch won the vote." The corrected version is stronger, and it's about the eldest sister specifically:**
> 1. **Ichika got the most if-chapters (2 in the exam + 2 in the festival = 4, more than anyone).**
> 2. **Her exam branch is the only one that needed two chapters.** Because hers is the branch where **she learns the truth about the photo girl and decides to compete** — the branch that *changes the game*, so it couldn't fit in one chapter like the others.
> 3. **Her exam branch is the only one the anime spread across two episodes** (59 in ep.7, 60 in ep.8). **They instinctively gave the biggest branch more runway.**
> 4. **Its branch contains the manga's single most-loved chapter, voted by ~7.7% of 89,191 voters, and it is the branch of the sister whose entire tragedy is that she was too mature to compete.** *(ja.wiki, verbatim on her character: 「気遣いが行き過ぎてしまい、自分自身の気持ちを押し殺してしまいがち」— "her consideration for others runs so deep that she tends to strangle her own feelings.")*
> **The most-loved chapter in a story about five equal girls belongs to the one who *voluntarily* took less. That's not a coincidence about popularity. That's the franchise telling you what readers actually reward: not the winner, not the loudest — the one whose love showed up as a *sacrifice the reader had to notice on their own.*** 🤓
> **FOR YOUR 24 CHAPTERS: this is a design instruction, not trivia.** Your Jesús Fujioka enters "at first" without displacing Fuutarou — **the mechanism of *voluntary* restraint.** The property's own data says **the reader's favourite material is whoever *chooses* not to take the page. So don't give Jesús equal chapters to apologize for his intrusion. Give him *fewer, at first* — and let the readers notice, exactly the way they noticed Ichika.** ⚠️ *And then reverse it structurally at the end, because that's what the manga did to Ichika and it's what broke the internet when it didn't do it clearly enough.*

**The spread is between roughly 5 and 10 chapters of "hers" per girl out of 122.** **That's a ratio of at most 2:1 — and the 2:1 gap is not a director's cruelty, it's that Nino's and Yotsuba's arcs are *serialized conflict* (many short beats) while Miku's and Itsuki's are *set pieces* (few long ones).** 🤓 **Read that again as craft, because it's the whole answer to "how do I give five girls equal treatment without writing the same chapter five times": you cannot make the *count* equal and the *story* good. You make the *function* equal — every sister gets (a) one private chapter, (b) one conflict-with-Fuutarou chapter, (c) one chapter where she changes the group, (d) one if-branch — and let the counts be different.** **That's the 五つ子ゲーム logic, and it's the fairness contract I'd put in your Ch. 1.**

**And the franchise's own fix, filed here so we don't re-derive it in §3:** for four years Kodansha's answer to "she got fewer chapters" was **「five separate books, one per girl, in a fixed order」** — the 2019–20 character books, the 2024 総集編 (一花の夢 03-15 / 二乃の気持ち 04-24 / 三玖の本気 05-15 / 四葉の秘密 06-12 / 五月の願い 07-10, ⚠️ **official @5Hanayome tweet: the release cadence was *exactly one month apart*, which is why I believe the schedule and not the description; and note the 5 volumes are a straight 14÷5 chop, "not a per-heroine episode collection" per namu.wiki**), the 2025 ANIME VISUAL BOOK set (一花/二乃/三玖/四葉/五月), and **both** game-adjacent 5-book campaigns. **They never balanced the manga. They balanced the *product line*.** 😜 **You have 24 chapters, not 5 books. So put the balance in the *order*: 4-4-4-4-4 across your five blocks, birth order, every block four chapters, and let the *contents* be wildly uneven. That's the move that's been hiding in the merchandise.**
