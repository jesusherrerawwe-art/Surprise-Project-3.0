> ### 🤓 THE FINDING THAT MATTERS MOST IN ALL OF SECTION TWO
> **The anime cut the Labor-Gratitude-Thanksgiving Tour — a *six-chapter block* (36–41) — and then, two and a half years later, made an OVA whose entire purpose was to come back and adapt chapter 38.**

**Read the shape of that. It is not "the studio made mistakes." It is:**
1. **At 12 episodes you cannot take everything. Something has to go, and what goes is what the *creators* can't see the value of yet.**
2. **Fans then canonized the discarded block anyway — as a *memory*.** Chapter 37, never animated, was voted **#7 of all 122** in the official election. ⚠️ **Correction mid-draft:** I first wrote that it "beat the finale by 4×." **I misread my own table — the finale (4,762) actually outpolled ch.37 (3,528).** The real finding is sharper and it's in §3b below.
3. **The publisher's eventual fix was not to redo the season. It was to release the missing scene as a *premium extra.***

**Three consequences for your 24 chapters, and I want you to actually decide on each:**
- **You are allowed to cut.** Kodansha cut a top-10 chapter and the property *thrived.* Perfectionism is not the virtue it looks like here.
- **But write down what you cut.** A "cut ledger" costs you five minutes and becomes, later, your most valuable asset: it's a list of **free canon you can hand out.** ∽ exists because someone kept a list.
- **Whatever you cut is *not* wasted if it is a *scene* rather than a *plot beat.*** They cut ch.38 — a pool date, emotionally load-bearing, narratively removable. They kept every plot beat. **The correct cut is a beautiful scene, never a necessary event.** ⚠️ If you cut a *necessary* event, the audience smells it as a hole; ∽ exists precisely because 36–41 contained a beat the fans decided the story required.

> 🔒 **ROUND-3 VERIFICATION (E41).** This row is now cross-checked against **four** independent contents lists — Kodansha's own partial Vol.7 TOC plus candygum, chill-middle-age and manga-games — and they agree: **55 三玖 · 56 四葉 · 57 五月 · 58 一花 · 59 二乃. One chapter each. Perfectly symmetric.** **ch.60「攻略開始」 is NOT a branch** and my "one branch spans two episodes" claim (which I pushed as a *correction* last round) is **dead — I was wrong twice before being right.**
> **The surviving finding is the sharper one, and it is about *position*, not screen time:** the anime placed the franchise's most beloved scene **as the first four minutes of an episode that then spent twenty on a different arc.** It did that for a structural reason — ch.59's consequences (the confession heard by the wrong sister) are the *engine* of ch.60–68 — **so Nino's branch was load-bearing for someone else's arc and could not be given its own container.** 🤓
> **Which hands you a rule I'd fight for: a chapter's *rank* in the reader's memory is set by what it *detonates*, not by how much page it owns.** You do not need to give your best sister the most chapters. **You need to give her the chapter that the next four cannot exist without — and then let it sit at the end of the run, not the middle.**
> ### 🤓 READ THE ASYMMETRY
> **The thing fans loved and the thing the production prioritized were almost perfectly *un-correlated*.** The production was optimizing for **forward motion of the plot** (whom will he marry → answer). The fans were optimizing for **the best *scene*** — and nine of the top twelve scenes in the entire 122-chapter story are **branch, if-route, or private-intimacy material**, not plot.
> **Now hold that against your own plan.** You have 24 chapters: **4 spine + 20 branch (4 per sister)** = **83% of your runtime inside the branches.** *The fans' favorite configuration — the exact thing the anime couldn't afford.* **Your structure is not a stylistic choice, it's the correction the franchise spent four years and one OVA trying to make.** That's the pitch sentence for the Game Plan, and it's the reason I'm confident this can *win* rather than merely please.
> **The warning that comes with it:** the anime also got *this* wrong — the fan-vote's #1 was given ¼ of an episode and the #3 got none. **If you give each sister four chapters and each one's is *merely pleasant*, you have built the same machine with better fuel.** The branches have to be *the best scenes in the book*, not the padding between plot beats. **Rank your 20 branch chapters against each other *before* drafting and put the top three in slots 8, 16 and 21** — where a reader's attention actually peaks. 😜
>
> ⚠️ *Caveat, because it's my inference and not a fact:* the vote was held **May 27–Jun 10, 2020, three months after the manga's finale and two years before S2 aired** — so readers were voting on the **manga**, unmediated by the anime. **It measures what the source material is loved for, not what the anime did well.** That's exactly why it's usable as a design target and *not* as a rating.

## 4. 🔥 THE OP LYRICS ARE A MASTERCLASS IN FIVE-VOICE WRITING

**「五等分のカタチ」**, lyrics/composition/arrangement entirely by **武田将弥 (Dream Monster)** — one person, all three roles, a "new talent" per the label.

The opening of the song is **five spoken lines, all meaning "tell me / teach me," each in a different register** [utaten](https://utaten.com/lyric/qk21017045/):

| # | Line | Register | Speaker |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 「ねぇ、教えてくれる？」 | casual, equal, soft ending | **Ichika** — the big sister who doesn't posture |
| 2 | 「ちょっと、教えなさいよ…」 | **imperative + よ**, the verb aimed *at* you | **Nino** — asks for nothing; commands |
| 3 | 「ここ、教えて？」 | clipped, minimal, no tail | **Miku** — the smallest possible request |
| 4 | 「～っ、教えてください！」 | **polite + exclamation**, breathless | **Yotsuba** |
| 5 | 「教えてくれますか？」 | **full formal interrogative** | **Itsuki** |

> ### 🤓 THIS IS YOUR ANSWER TO "HOW DO I WRITE FIVE IDENTICAL PEOPLE"
> **Same verb. Same meaning. Same syllable budget. Five different human beings.**
> No name is spoken. No color is mentioned. Nothing visual. **Pure grammar.**
> The lyricist solved, in twenty seconds of audio, the exact problem your novel exists to solve — and he solved it **without description.** Kevin, when you design your Jesús-Hana-Fuutarou voices and your five sister-POVs: **the test is not "could a reader picture her?" The test is "could a reader identify her from a single line of dialogue with no dialogue tag and no adjective." That OP passes. Write until yours does.**
> The 「」bracketed-line-as-identification trick is *directly* portable to prose: **open each of your five branch-blocks with a five-line exchange of one shared sentence, and let the branch be *named* by the register rather than by the girl.** 😜 *(Also, and I refuse not to say it: the song counts 1・2・3・4・5 down the verses with each sister's reason attached to her number — 「単純な答えじゃない / 好きの好きの好きの好きの好きのカタチは」 — and the line 「**好きな色はみんな違っても 同じ人に惹かれ**」 "even though the colors we like are all different, we were drawn to the same person" is the thesis of the property stated outright, in a pop song, for anyone who bothered to read the lyric sheet. Read it aloud.)*

**And a structural gem:** the closing couplet **「一緒でも変わる 好きのカタチ」** — *even the same becomes different* — **is the one thing the anime never managed to say, and the song said it first.**

### The lyricist's own statement of purpose (Pony Canyon interview, verbatim)
> 🤓 **「今回のオープニングは、1期のときでは歌えなかった歌詞が並んでいるなという印象があります。最初のセリフのところも、1期のときはみんなどこか風太郎に対して壁があったのが、今回は人によっては恋に落ちたような声になったりしていて、それは2期になったからこそできるアプローチだなとも思うので」**
> *"This opening has lyrics in it that they **couldn't sing in season one.** Even in the spoken intro — in S1 there was a wall between each of them and Fuutarou; this time some of them sound like they've *fallen.* That approach was only available because it's season two."*
> **The tie-in song is being engineered as a narrative signal by the record label, and the singers say so out loud.** Your Ch.-by-Ch. "season 1 vs season 2" tonal shift is legitimate franchise practice. Also note the killer lyric: 「**五等分のじゃ嫌だから 独り占めしてもいいかな**」 — *"being one-fifth isn't enough, is it alright if I take you all to myself?"* — the thesis sentence. **A girl explicitly rejecting equality.** If one line had to summarize this entire property, that's the line.

## 5. THE SALES: S2 LOST 40% OF ITS AUDIENCE AND IT DIDN'T MATTER

| Release | First week | Cumulative | Δ vs S1 same volume |
|---|---|---|---|
| S2 Vol.1 (eps 1–3) | **BD 2,917 + DVD 576 = 3,493** | **4,886** (12 wks) | vs S1 v1's **8,801** → **−44%** |
| S2 Compact Collection | — | **4,780** | (S1: 4,704) |
| **∽** (2 eps, 2023) | **11,808** *(one ledger lists a lower figure; 15,834 = BD+DVD)* | **~15,834** | **a single release outsold S2's *entire season*** |
| **∗** (2 eps, 2025) | DVD 748 / BD 4,346 | **~5,583** | **−65% vs ∽** |

**Reading (⚠️ my own inference):**
- **S2's first volume is *lower* than S1's *fifth* (4,292).** The season didn't decline gradually — it declined *immediately.*
- **The physical market for this title had already moved to "buy the event, not the series."** By 2023 nobody bought TV anime discs; they bought **the thing that ends the story.** ∽ = 3× S2's whole season. The film (see Doc 3) = **59,451**, ~1.9× the two *seasons combined.*
- **The 2022 Compact Collections — a full season for one price, released *four months after the film* — sold roughly as many units as each five-volume season *had*, individually.** Kodansha/Pony Canyon's real lesson: **packaging a season as one object sells as well as releasing it as five.**
- ⚠️ **The ∗ collapse is the counter-lesson.** Same length, same cast, same fanbase, same label, **fourteen months after a successful special** — and it earned a third. **The difference is that by then the story was over.** *Scarcity value requires an unresolved question.*
> **For your project, stated plainly: this means the *contest entry* is where the money is, not the serialized chapters.** Not because you should hoard, but because that's empirically where this audience puts its wallets — they pay for **the answer.** So: distribute the mystery freely and make **the finale unmissable.** And don't do what ∗ did — releasing an epilogue into a question that's already answered. 🤓

## 6. THE ONE THING S2 DID RIGHT THAT NOBODY CREDITS IT FOR

S1 skipped the fireworks chapter (ch.8) because the *block* was being compressed. **S2's most aggressive cuts were to ch.36–41 — the 勤労感謝ツアー and the living-room confession — and yet those six chapters are where the *if-route* structure was being born.** S2 protected, against its own time budget, **the three IF-exam chapters (55–58) and the Sisters' War (78–86)**, at the cost of the *confession* material.

> 🤓 **Verdict: S2's producers correctly intuited that the *mechanism* (fair branches) was the brand and that *confessions* were disposable. They were right about the brand and wrong about the fans** — the fandom canonized ch.37, a tour episode, not a plot machine. **The lesson for you is the uncomfortable version: the thing you think is "structural, therefore safe to trim" may be the exact thing people will love.** **I'd protect a chapter that is *warm* over one that is merely *clever.* Your 24 chapters have 4 in the spine for cleverness. Give the sisters warmth.**

### S2's verdict — copy / refuse
**COPY:** the **all-Ōchi single-voice scripting** (12/12 eps by one writer vs S1's 10/12 — the tonal consistency S2 has and S1 flirts with losing is likely *this*, not the animation); **the character-song mini-album** (five solos = the franchise's own per-sister format, and it was a *S2* addition); **「五等分のカタチ」's register-only identification.**
**REFUSE:** the **3.8 ch/ep rate**; **changing character designer between seasons of the same story** (⚠️ a five-identical-sisters property re-drew its faces mid-series — the one show on earth that could *not* afford that); and **cutting confession scenes to protect plot.**
**FLAGGED:** ch.44/45 ep3↔ep4 boundary; ep8's 63–68 vs 64–67; ep10's ch.73; ep12's 82–86 vs 83–86; S1 ep6's ch.13. **⚠️ None of these are settled by my sources and I refuse to pick one silently.** Do not cite a specific chapter↔episode pairing to a judge without checking the tankōbon table of contents directly; Kodansha's own product pages (e.g. [Vol.14](https://www.kodansha.co.jp/comic/products/0000337853), whose TOC I verified: ch.114–122 with the final chapter = 122) are the authority. 🤓
