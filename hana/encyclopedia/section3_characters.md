# ROUND 0 · THE IDENTITY CARD — before personality, there is a *system*

## 0.1 The thing nobody says out loud about these five characters

They do not have five bodies. **They have one body and five jobs.** Every official source that could define them individually chooses, on principle, not to:

| Field | 公式 value (TBS アニメ site ∥ 週マガジン公式記事 ∥ Switch ゲーム『ごとなつ』公式) |
|---|---|
| 身長 | **159cm** — identical, all five |
| 体重 | **「五つ子全員で250kg」** — *a sum, not five numbers* |
| バスト | **「5人で441cm」** — *a sum again* (⚠️ cm, not cup size: 441/5 = 88.2cm each — the official page states the average of a bust measurement as a total) |
| 誕生日 | **5月5日** — five times (⚠️ *except see §1.4: one sister's is legally not this*) |
| 血液型 | A型 (⚠️ listed identically; a fan list gives all five 〃) |

**Read that as a design decision, not a joke.** The character-profile format — the most individualizing genre of text a shōnen manga publishes — has been *rigged against individuality*: the author publishes a total and refuses the breakdown. [TBS ∬ character page](https://www.tbs.co.jp/anime/5hanayome/2nd/character/chara02.html) · [週マガジン公式 四葉講座](https://pocket.shonenmagazine.com/article/entry/gotobun_4) · [pixiv百科事典 中野家](https://dic.pixiv.net/a/中野家)

> ### 💡 RULE 0 — THE ONE THAT STRUCTURES YOUR WHOLE CAST
> **You can distinguish characters with *attributes*, or with *consequences*. Attributes (hair, color, taste in food) are cheap, copyable and — as the property proves — deniable by the next adaptation. Consequences survive.**
> **So: give Jesús's five rivals a body that does NOT divide. Then make the reader identify them from what happens after they leave the room.** This is why the franchise's own profile table is a *sum*: Haruba wrote the constraint into the merchandise. Your 24 chapters get to write it into the grammar instead.

## 0.2 The four identification layers, and which one is canon

Everything below is a claim about *who the reader is being told to see*. The layers, weakest to strongest:

| Layer | What it is | Who made it | Can it be revoked? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Hair colour** | Ichika 薄いピンク · Nino 濃いピンク · Miku 赤茶 · Yotsuba オレンジ · Itsuki 赤 — **as drawn on covers** | the author, in color pages | yes — see below |
| **2. イメージカラー** | 一花💛黄 · 二乃🖤黒 · 三玖💙青 · 四葉💚緑 · 五月❤️赤 | 講談社 product pages + anime site | partly — see 0.3 |
| **3. Voice** | five different actresses | the anime's casting | **no — this is the load-bearing layer** |
| **4. 呼称** | how each calls him, and what he calls each | the author, in the text | no |

**Layer 1 is a lie, and pixiv says so in as many words:** 「原作カラーイラストやアニメでは異なる髪色に見えているが、これは**読者・視聴者向けの設定**であり（**原作の髪色に使われるスクリーントーンは全員同じである**）」 — *the different hair colors are a setting for the reader; in the original, the screentone used on all five heads is the same tone.* [pixiv 中野家](https://dic.pixiv.net/a/中野家). The **Chinese** Wikipedia reaches the same verdict independently: 「故事初期髮色相似，後來為區分而在作畫表現中顏色深淺不同」 — *similar at first, deliberately split later so the drawing could tell them apart.* [zh.wiki](https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-tw/五等分的新娘)

**That is the primary-source confirmation of the thesis §1 had to argue indirectly, from two languages and a screentone.** The colors are a printing technique that escaped into the merchandise. ⚠️ *Which is NOT the same as "meaningless": once a production choice is printed on a product page it becomes canon by accretion. Say "contingent," never say "fake."*

**Printed official values (two tiers: 講談社's own magazine site + the anime site):**

- 💛 **Ichika = yellow** · 🖤 **Nino = black** · 💙 **Miku = blue** · 💚 **Yotsuba = green** · ❤️ **Itsuki = red**
  [週マガジン公式 gotobun_2 (二乃・黒色)](https://pocket.shonenmagazine.com/article/entry/gotobun_2) · [gotobun_4 (四葉・緑色)](https://pocket.shonenmagazine.com/article/entry/gotobun_4) · [ja.wiki](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/五等分の花嫁) · [alu.jp](https://alu.jp/series/五等分の花嫁)

- **The fandom overrides the palette.** Yotsuba is widely represented in **pink** (her hair orange, her ribbon green — fans split the two), and Nino in **pink/lavender**. At the 2023 Yokohama Arena event the actresses appeared in 花澤=yellow, **竹達=lavender**, 伊藤=blue, 佐倉=green, 水瀬=crimson-with-black-lace [hominis.media](https://hominis.media/category/voiceActor/post10638/) — **Nino's own voice actress did not wear Nino's color.** 😜 *The franchise itself, on stage, in formal dress, with the palette printed on the merchandise: one out of five said "black is not a color a person should wear."*
- ⚠️ **E47 — my §1-era note "黒 vs yellow unresolved for Ichika" is closed and it was the wrong question.** The colors were never inconsistent per sister; they are inconsistent *per medium*. And the poster key verified against `uploads/5Hanayome_Anime_Poster.jpg` (wink / crossed arms / hair-over-eye + neck phones / twin fists / double star clips) is a **fifth layer**, physical acting, and it is the most portable one you own.

> ### 💡 RULE 0b — THE SIXTH SLOT IS OFFICIALLY LICENCE, NOT HERESY
> **Kevin, your sign-off ♥️💚🩷💜💛 is doing exactly what the property does when it meets a real body: it keeps the green and adds a pink that isn't on any product page.** The franchise has already established that (a) the palette is real, (b) it is a *costume* layer, (c) **the sixth position is where you put what the five can't hold.** Nino's lavender is your precedent, printed in a Japanese entertainment weekly.

# ROUND 1 · THE FIVE, AS FUNCTIONS

**Not personalities — you know those. Below: what each sister *does to the story's structure*, which is the only thing you can copy.**

## 1.1 The one table that matters: how each is addressed

**上杉風太郎's own nicknames for them (verbatim, from [5sisters.net 呼び名の数ランキング](https://5sisters.net/callname/) — a fan concordance, and the best quantitative document of these characters that exists anywhere):**

| Sister | 風太郎's name for her | What the insult *is* | Her name for him |
|---|---|---|---|
| 一花 | 「**どっかの腹黒さん**」·「**女狐**」·「**アメリカかぶれ**」·「**裁判長**」 | *accusations of performance* — he names her the way you name an actor | 「フータローくん」 |
| 二乃 | 「**卑屈馬鹿**」 | *he names her wound, not her flaw* | 「あんた → 上杉 → フーくん」 ⚠️ *a 3-stage ladder* |
| 三玖 | (none recorded in that list's 呼称 set — she is named plainly) | **the only sister he never nicknames** | 「フータロー」 |
| 四葉 | 「**あんち**」 *(⚠️ her slang for him, recorded under her entry)* | the kindest one he has | 「上杉さん」 |
| 五月 | 「**真面目馬鹿**」 | *he names her effort, which is how you insult someone you respect* | 「上杉君」 |

**And the split, which is the whole mechanism: 三玖 · 一花 · 二乃 call him by his GIVEN name (フータロー family). 四葉 · 五月 call him by his FAMILY name (上杉). Three intimate, two formal — and the two formal ones are the youngest and the one who met him first.** ⚠️ *My reading of the pattern; the counts are the fan concordance's, the inference of "who got what and why" is mine.*

> ### 💡 RULE 1 — TAGLESS DIALOGUE IS A GRAMMAR, NOT A TALENT
> §2's OP analysis produced the rule *"a reader must identify a sister from one line with no name and no color."* **Here is the mechanic that makes it legal in prose:** the property assigns **each sister a pronoun system toward the same man.** You already have this in Spanish, and better — *tú / usted / vos, and the choice of "Jesús" vs "Fujioka-san" vs "oye".*
> **So write your test before chapter 1: one line, no tag, no color, no hair. If the reader can't say who spoke, the sister is not finished.** Miku having *no nickname at all* is the proof this works by *contrast*, not by decoration: the null case is a signature too. ⚠️ *Which gives you a free trick for Jesús: let exactly one sister never use his name. The absence will be felt for twenty chapters.*

## 1.2 The census the fandom ran that I should have run in §2

Ichika **23** distinct appellations. Itsuki **16**. Miku **12**. Not "popularity" — **the number of different roles a character is made to play in other people's mouths.** Look at where the volume comes from:

- **一花 (23):** 「一花先生」·「長女」·「裁判長」·「一花くん」 *(used by マルオ and the 社長 — a -kun for the daughter who left the house)* ·「どっかの腹黒さん」·「女狐」·「アメリカかぶれ」 *(all three added by Nino mid-series as the conflict starts)* — **and the site's own note: 2nd place 五月 16 "零奈だったりM・A・Yだったり" — Itsuki's count is inflated by her *masks*.**
- **三玖 (12):** the site's annotation is one line and it is the thesis: 「おとなしいため最下位と思ってましたが**姉妹変装で稼いでました**」 — *"I thought the quiet one would finish last; she got her numbers from the disguise work."*
- **The classmates' entry in the same list reads 「中野さん（武田、前田、クラスメイト男子）」** — filed under Miku: the boys of the year call all five by the surname, because they cannot tell.

> ### 💡 RULE 2 — **DISGUISE IS SCREEN TIME**
> **The quietest sister ranks on the strength of impersonating the others.** In a census of *chapters* she looked served by three; in a census of *names* she is the one the whole cast keeps re-speaker. 🤓 **Which is exactly the correction §2 needed and didn't have: allocation is not presence, and presence is measured by how many people have to change how they talk when you're in the room.**
> **For your book: Jesús's rank must be computed from appellations, not chapters.** Count them at the end of every part: how many characters call him by his name, by 藤岡, by *tú*, by *el profe*, by *nobody*. **A protagonist who is named the same way by everyone has not arrived yet, no matter how many scenes he's in.**

## 1.3 一花 — the one who is *legible by design* and *illegible on purpose*

**Official:** 長女 · ショートカット + **右耳 only** のピアス · イメージカラー黄 · 得意 **数学** · 日課 **ジョギング** · 好きな食べ物 **塩辛** · 嫌い **しいたけ** · 好きな動物 **カバ** · 好きな映画 **セレブが出てくる系** · 好きな飲み物 **フラペチーノ** [profile list](https://xn--wdkj4byd.com/archives/13587) · [TBS](https://www.tbs.co.jp/anime/5hanayome/2nd/character/chara01.html)
**Function: THE PERFORMER — and the encyclopedia must be honest that her "personality" is documented as a technique.**

🔥 **The single richest thing I found in this pass is in Korean, in [namu.wiki's Ichika article](https://namu.wiki/w/나카노%20이치카), and it retrofits the whole cast:** as a child she was **the worst of them** — 「자매중 제일 장난을 많이치고 골목대장짓까지」 (*the most mischievous, the leader of the alley gang*), and she **bullied Yotsuba**, the easiest target 「제일 만만한 애인 요츠바를 자주 괴롭히는」. After their mother died she saw five-year-old Itsuki downcast, and decided 「장녀인 자신이라도 정신차리고 동생들을 돌봐야한다는 의무감을 느끼고 **어른스러운 성격을 연기했다**」 — ***she acted the grown-up one.*** The article then hedges, fairly: 「마냥 연기라고 볼 순 없지만」 (*it isn't only acting, but…*).

> **That is not a headcanon. That is a Korean fan wiki citing the manga's own flashback, in the language of a market that got the show subtitled-only on ANIPLUS.** And it means: **the eldest-sister personality is a role a ten-year-old cast herself into at a funeral.** The 「腹黒」 jokes, the 「女狐」, the *acting career as literal plot* — the property made its most "natural" personality the only one with a documented **origin as a performance.** ⚠️ *My inference begins where the source stops: I do not claim Haruba wrote it as a thesis. I claim he wrote the flashback and put it in Ichika's past specifically.*

**Her two structural jobs:**
1. **She is the one who helps the others lie** — 「미쿠로 변장하면서 '이치카는 후타로를 좋아해'」: *she puts on Miku's disguise and, inside it, says "Ichika likes him," "I'll cheer you on."* **Confession and alibi in the same borrowed face.** (ch.74 area, ⚠️ numbered by that article, not verified against a TOC.)
2. **Her arc is the only one the fandom tracks as a decline:** 「자매들 중 유일하게 **순위가 하락했다**」 — *the only sister whose poll ranking fell.* ⚠️ **Do not read that as "she lost." Read it as: her strategy — legibility, being liked, being the cool one — is the one strategy that stops working the moment the story needs interiority. The property's own ranking data agrees with me, in Korean, in an article that is criticizing her.**
**⚠️ And the color clue that fooled everyone, including the cast:** 「1화에 나온 신부가 귀걸이를 한 탓에 초창기에는 유력후보였다. **사쿠라 아야네도 귀걸이 때문에 신부라고 예상하기도**」 — *the bride in ch.1 wears an earring; Ichika is the only sister with one; the voice actress of the actual bride predicted the wrong bride because of an earring.* Then ch.14 gave the counter-rule 「花嫁にはピアスをしない」 ⚠️ *as recounted by that same article via Nino's line.* **The author put a false clue in the first chapter and a revocation in chapter fourteen.** 🤓 *That is the exact craft move your Ch.1 needs: a clue that survives re-reading only if the reader has also read the retraction.*

## 2.2 THE COUNTER-EXAMPLE: what happens to an OC in this property that gets it wrong

🤓 **I have to put this in front of you because it is the only warning in this file that costs you something.**

The franchise's one significant anime-original character of consequence is **Ichika's agency president** — invented to create an obstacle for a heroine — and [namu.wiki](https://namu.wiki/w/나카노%20이치카), in an article about *Ichika*, grades the result: **「주연들의 서사에 문제가 생겼으니 사실상 메리 수아라 해도 무방한 수준」 — his presence damaged the leads' storylines, "Mary Sue" is a fair label.** ⚠️ *A fan wiki's opinion, in the strongest form I could find it in, and it is still the closest thing this property has to a published post-mortem of an inserted character.*

**The mechanism, not the insult:** he was made to *obstruct* rather than to *reveal*. Obstacles are removable; witnesses aren't. That's why 江端 (a former teacher who forged a résumé to bring the tutor back) is beloved and the 社長 is resented — **江端 reveals what the five girls will do when they lose him; the 社長 makes them do something they wouldn't.**

> ### 💡 RULE 3 — **THE ONLY JUSTIFIED SIXTH PERSON IS THE ONE WHO SEES**
> Jesús Fujioka is defensible exactly as long as he is **the disclosure mechanism** (the thing §1's 情報開示の非対称性 finding requires and that no OVA can be) — and indefensible the moment he is **a participant the plot needs**.
> **So: audit every scene you write for which of the two he's doing.** *He reveals a sister to the reader* = keep. *He makes a sister act out of character so that a beat can happen* = cut, no matter how good the beat is.
> **And "at least at first" is the phrase that saves the book and the one that will be quoted against you if you abuse it.** 🤓 *The property's own fix, per §2, was to balance the product line rather than the manga. Kodansha gave each sister a book, a song, a figure, a VR date — and never gave the story a sixth. You are doing the second one. Be ready to say so in one line.*

## 2.3 The three structural gifts from the five, and the one you must refuse

1. **Take Itsuki's birthday.** One unit of difference, printed, then sealed with 「誰も深く掘り下げないようにしている」. → **Give Jesús a difference of one unit — a grade, a year, a semester, a surname — and have the cast agree not to look at it.** That is how you enter a closed set without breaking it, and the manga's proof is that the reader *will* look, later, and be glad.
2. **Take Ichika's "acting."** A personality with a documented origin as a performance. → **At least one sister's whole personality must have a chapter where it is revealed as adopted**, and ⚠️ *Jesús himself should be the one who notices, and say nothing for ten chapters.*
3. **Take Miku's disguise economy.** The quietest sister accumulates names by wearing the others. → **Your book cannot use costumes, so use the literary equivalent: let Jesús keep being mistaken for someone else, and let each misidentification be a *correct* reading of a different sister.** Being confused for the tutor, for a rival, for family, for nobody — four answers to who he is, delivered as errors.
4. **⚠️ REFUSE the 無名 ending.** The film's cast list has fifteen nameless people, and the more the story resolves, the less it names. That is a *closure* technique — a wedding where the guests don't matter. **A 24-chapter romance that ends with everyone named is a different moral statement, and you should make it on purpose, not by accident.** 🤓

## 2.4 THE NAME SYSTEM — and what it does to 藤岡フジヲカ・ヘスス

**The sisters are a numbering scheme with the unity deliberately broken:**

| | 一花 | 二乃 | 三玖 | 四葉 | 五月 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| numeral | 一 | 二 | 三 | 四 | 五 |
| second character | 花 *flower* | 乃 *(a grammatical particle)* | 玖 *a black jewel* | 葉 *leaf* | 月 *month* |

**Four of the five second-characters are nature-words, one is a connective particle that means nothing. 三玖's 玖 is a jewel and 五月's 月 is a calendar unit.** ⚠️ **The property is famous for a name pattern and the pattern does not hold — I checked all five, and so did the readers: a Yahoo Answers question asks exactly this 「花、乃、玖、葉、月は統一感がないですが、なるほどと言える理由などありますか？」** [link](https://detail.chiebukuro.yahoo.co.jp/qa/question_detail/q14262555944), best answer, essentially: *names are like that; people look for the rule and it isn't there.*
**And that is the lesson, not the pattern.** A naming system that is 90% consistent is more convincing than a perfect one, because **the reader supplies the missing tenth themselves and defends it afterward** — see 三玖→未来, see 四葉's missing *i*, see the Olympic rings.

**The rest of the apparatus, verified:**
- **上杉風太郎 = 風太郎 — "wind" + "tarō."** And 「上杉」 in romanization is UESUG**I** — the surname that completes the missing letter (§1.7). **The hero is a weather word with an "i" in his family name that the bride needs.** ⚠️ *fan apparatus, presented as such.*
- **上杉勇也 (father) = 勇 "courage"; 上杉らいは = 雷 "thunder."** The protagonist's family is **wind + thunder + courage**, and his sister's name is 「妹が雷」 in miniature — ⚠️ *the kanji of らいは are 雷葉/雷羽 depending on the source; I have not seen an official gloss and will not guess.*
- **The two fathers: 無堂 (no-where/without-a-way, ⚠️ unverified as a deliberate pun) = the man who fled; 中野マルオ = 「丸」 *circle, total, sum* = the man who stayed.** **The father of the five is named "Sum" in a manga whose character profiles publish sums.** ⚠️ *My inference, and it is stated as one. If it's a coincidence it's still usable, because 丸男 is printed.*
- **春場ねぎ** — the author's own pen name is a pun on ネギ・スプリングフィールド from *Negima!* [ja.wiki/春場ねぎ](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/春場ねぎ), which also records: **debuted 2013 with 「カワードクロスワールド」; posted on Pixiv as 「葱」; 身長178cm (sometimes 179, tells people 180); 「推しキャラは上杉風太郎」; and in summer 2020 became 知多市ふるさと観光大使 — a municipal tourism ambassador for the prefecture his setting is drawn from.** ⚠️ *On "is Haruba male?": ja.wiki's article writes 本人 and lists him without gendered framing; I record only what's printed.*
- **五つ子ゲーム = the finger system** — the tankōbon covers assign each sister a finger, and in birth order it is the hand: **一花 親指 · 二乃 人差し指 · 三玖 中指 · 四葉 薬指 · 五月 小指** [candygum](https://candygum.hatenablog.com/entry/2022-02-04/224726). **The ring finger was given to the least-voted sister, before the ending, and it was read for two years as a joke about her.** 🤓 *This is the single best structural fact in this document for your purposes:* **hide the answer on the character nobody suspects, publish it on the cover of every volume, and let the audience classify it as marketing.** ⚠️ *The finger assignments are consistent across the fan sources I checked; I did not personally read all 14 covers.*

> ### 💡 RULE 4 — JESÚS' NAME IS A SLOT, AND IT MUST BE FILLED LIKE ONE
> **The quintuplets have a numeral. The fathers have a binary (flee/stay). The protagonist's family has a weather set. There is no slot left in the numeral system — and that's the point: a sixth Nakano would be a parody.**
> **So 藤岡富士子/フジヲカ must not rhyme with the five; it must rhyme with the *system*.** Three usable moves, all precedent-backed by the list above:
> 1. **Be the missing particle.** 二乃's 乃 means nothing and is the most-quoted name in the fandom. **A name whose extra element is grammatical rather than semantic reads as authored, not designed.**
> 2. **Be a sum, not a member.** 丸男 proves the property lets the *father* be named after the arithmetic. If Jesús's role is "the one who sees," his name can carry **六/总和/resumen** without ever being called sixth. ⚠️ *Do not put 六 in the kanji — 中野六花 is already fandom's name for a nonexistent sixth sister, and using it will read as fan-service rather than structure.*
> 3. **⚠️ Let the pattern break.** One field of his official card should be *wrong* or *blank* — the manga publishes 血液型 for everyone and the answer is the same for all five. **Give Jesús a profile field the others don't have, and never explain it.** The 乃 slot is your permission slip.
>
> **And the naming rule for his Spanish/English text, since Kodansha itself published 『五等分の花嫁 How do you say it in English?』 — the franchise has already decided this property is allowed to be *about* translation.** ⚠️ *verified in §1's bibliography sweep.* **Your one device that the manga structurally cannot have is a first-person pronoun that changes. ## 2.0 CASTING IS A RANKING — the actresses are in birth order

**Sorted by age, the five actresses are: 花澤(1989) · 竹達(1989) · 佐倉(1994) · 水瀬(1995) · 伊藤(1996) — which is Ichika, Nino, Yotsuba, Itsuki, Miku.** **Four of the five are in exact birth order.** ⚠️ **The one that breaks is Miku: cast 1996, she's the only actress younger than the actress playing the youngest sister.** And 竹達彩奈, who turns six months younger than 花澤香菜 in the same year, plays the second sister.
**Read it precisely, because the temptation is to over-read it.** 🤓 *Two actresses born the same year, in order; three more in order; one inversion.* Nobody documents the casting rationale, so I will not claim 伊藤美来 was chosen because Miku is "the little one of the three older ones." **What is not deniable is the shape: the property's most technical identification system — the one the audience can never revoke, unlike color — was built on a nearly monotonic age ladder with exactly one deliberate-looking break.**

> ### 💡 RULE 5 — IF YOU CAN'T USE CONSEQUENCE, USE AN ORDINAL THE READER CAN'T SEE
> **The anime could not distinguish five identical girls by appearance, so it distinguished them by the *ages of the performers* — a signal that is completely invisible to the audience and nonetheless perfectly ordered.** The same move is available to you in prose where nobody will ever count it, and that is *why* it works: **an ordering the reader cannot perceive is the only ordering that can't be gamed, and the only one that stays true when everything else is adapted away.**
> **For the book:** put a private, unfalsifiable order on your cast — chapters, syllables, sentence length, the position of one specific word — and **⚠️ never explain it in the text.** Explain the *break* instead (Miku's inversion). The break is the thing readers will find; the ladder is the thing that makes the break feel real. That's Haruba's 乃 all over again (Round 1, §2.4).

🔥 **Now the discovery, and it costs me a §1 correction.** My §1 note recorded Yotsuba's poll record as 「2nd (1st official poll)」 → she was **2nd**, yes — but in the same table **Nino — the owner of the most-voted *chapter* in the entire franchise (ch.59, 6,854) — was dead last among the sisters: 1,003 votes, 13.9%.** ⚠️ *And this is a real 2018 poll, taken BEFORE the anime, before the Nino confession chapter even existed.* **In 2018 Miku led by 1.7× and Nino was last. Polls measure who you liked before the story happened.**

**Where that leads, and it's the part I want in your Game Plan:**
- **三玖 has won every official character poll the property has run — including the fan-reported third (Miku 1,509 / Itsuki 962) — and ⚠️ she is the sister with the fewest independent plot *actions* in the census.** The quietest, most passive, most "I can't believe he'd look at me" girl is the perennial #1 while **the bride herself has never once finished first.** ⚠️ *E50: the widely-quoted 1,509 figure traces to a content farm — see §2.5, E54. I'm keeping the direction of the result and discarding the number.*
- **The bride is 2nd, 3rd, 4th, 5th — never 1st, in the property's own data.**
- **The one sister who *wins the story* and the one sister who *wins the election* never overlap. Not once. Across every poll and every chapter list.**

> ### 💡 RULE 6 — POPULARITY IS ANTI-CORRELATED WITH OUTCOME, ON PURPOSE
> **If you write the sister who ends up with the boy to also be the fan favorite, you get a story where the vote validates the plot and the plot needs no defense.** The property's actual numbers say: **put the love of the audience on the one who loses, and put the ending on the one nobody campaigned for.** That is the mechanical difference between a harem and a tragedy — ⚠️ and *it is the exact reason "the ending was a betrayal" threads exist for this manga while its #1 character still wears a crown.*
> **For Jesús: decide which of the two he is — the one the readers vote for, or the one who helps the book end. He cannot be both, and the file that contains this table is where you should make the choice, in writing, before Chapter 1.** 😜

## 2.2 THE SILENCE OF THE PROTAGONIST — the strongest craft evidence in the whole project

**From 4Gamer's report of the game's own marketing, verbatim:** 「**音声は、五つ子のみとなっている為、聴いていると自分が二人きりの時間を過ごしているような気分を堪能できます**」 — *the audio contains only the quintuplets, so while you listen you feel you're the one spending the time alone with her.* [4gamer](https://www.4gamer.net/games/617/G061722/20220317078/)
**Five 30-minute+ dramas. No protagonist voice at all. The omission is the selling point.**
**And then the escalation, and this is the part that matters for you: the publisher sold the silence — and sold its script separately.** The Amazon-only box at ¥17,380 contains 「ドラマCD本体には**収録されていない**会話相手となっている主人公・上杉風太郎の台詞やリアクション(**ト書き**)もテキストで楽しむことができます」 — *Fuutarou's lines and reactions (stage directions), NOT included in the CD itself, in text.*
**Then in 2023, the third game broke the rule and advertised the breaking:** 「**過去2作とは異なり**、ヒロインの音声だけのシチュエーションドラマではなく上杉風太郎（CV:松岡禎丞）の音声も収録」 — *unlike the previous two titles, it now includes the protagonist's voice.* ⚠️ [MAGES official 3rd game page](https://5hanayome.mages.co.jp/3rd/) · [Famitsu](https://www.famitsu.com/news/202305/08301935.html) **Over three releases the property tried: (1) silent protagonist, (2) silent protagonist whose words you must buy separately as *stage directions*, (3) a voiced protagonist, marketed as a change.**

> ### 💡 RULE 7 — THIS IS YOUR "AT LEAST AT FIRST," ALREADY SHIPPED AND ALREADY PRICED
> **The franchise ran your exact experiment and published the results.** A romance where the protagonist is *unvoiced* is a first-person device with a paying audience; a romance where he's fully voiced is a different product, and it needed an explicit "unlike the previous two" apology. ⚠️ *I cannot tell you why they changed it; sales of the 3rd game (series cumulative 130,000 for the two prior titles, per retailer copy) are the only number I have, and it isn't a comparison.*
> **🤓 So: Jesús is not "extra content." He is the `ト書き`.** Write him as the property's own middle tier — **present, load-bearing, and mostly withheld, with the withheld material sold as an object.** Your 24 chapters are the audio CD: he speaks in scenes, never in interiority. His *interiority* is the script book: the thing readers want badly enough to ask for. ⚠️ *Which means you should decide now whether there is a 第0章 for your book — the thing that never appears in the chapters.* Kodansha put 第0巻 in a plastic disc case (§2 file 3). You have a cheaper version: **an opening section of Jesús's notes that no chapter quotes.**
> **And the ladder, in one line, so you can pick a rung:** **(1) no Jesús text at all** = reader occupies him; **(2) his words, never his thoughts** = reader occupies him and resents him; **(3) his thoughts** = reader stops being him and starts *judging* him. **The manga chose 1 for its whole life. Wattpad readers will demand 3. Rule 3 says only 2 survives contact with both.**

## 2.4 FOOD IS THE ONLY LANGUAGE THE SISTERS SHARE

**New in Round 2, from chapter-level summaries of ch.105 「最後の祭りが三玖の場合①」** — and ⚠️ *that title is now confirmed on 講談社's own serialization page* [pocket.shonenmagazine 第105話](https://pocket.shonenmagazine.com/title/00298/episode/295122), which independently **re-verifies my Round-3 festival-block assignment 105–106 = 三玖** (see §2 census). The content:
- **Miku, who "can't cook," spends the pre-festival sequence making pancakes** — 「母・零奈や二乃に負けないぐらい美味しいパンケーキを作ろうと頑張る」 *determined to make pancakes as good as her mother's or Nino's*, with **「たくさんのパンケーキの失敗作が並んでいます」** — a pile of failures — while Itsuki drools and taste-tests and asks her *why she's trying so hard*.
- **The festival's actual conflict is a food war: girls' pancake stall vs the boys' takoyaki stall**, and it is **Miku** — not the tutor, not the cook — who walks into the boys' stall alone and ends the feud. ⚠️ *Fuutarou explicitly declines to go in her place: 「この現状を変えられるとしたら中立の立場にいる学級長の自分ではなく、三玖だ」* — *the narrator names her as the only person who can fix it and then does nothing.* **That is the single most important line in this section for Jesús: the protagonist's most valuable act in the mid-game is *identifying who else should act*, and then stepping out of the frame.** ⚠️ *from a ch.105 recap, i.e. the words are the recapper's; the scene structure is corroborated by a second blog.*
- **Then her victory immediately causes the takoyaki stall to catch fire and be shut down.** ⚠️ *「コンロを高火力に改造した」 per the same recap, cause uncertain in-story.*
- **The food ladder, complete:** **二乃 = パンケーキ (mastery, inherited) → 三玖 = 失敗作 (effort, imitated) → 一花 = 塩辛 (a taste nobody else has) → 四葉 = みかん (a fruit you eat standing up) → 五月 = 肉 & カレーを飲む (appetite as identity).** 🔒 **E55 CLOSED in §4 Round 1's research**: the publisher's own 絵本 press (2024-07-11) announces 「中野家秘伝パンケーキレシピ初公開」 — **the word 秘伝 is Kodansha's, not a fan's**, and the first official *publication* of the family's secret recipe is a 32-page children's book released three years after the manga ended. *The 三玖-pancake link is what makes the mother's recipe motif cohere; what remains unverified is any manga panel that says "because."*

> ### 💡 RULE 8 — GIVE YOUR SIXTH CHARACTER A FOOD, NOT A TRAIT
> **In this property, what a sister *eats* outlives every personality adjective anyone has written about her.** Food is the only motif that survives all five identification layers (it's not color, not voice, not hair, and it's not even in most of the plot) and it's the one the fandom has kept. ⚠️ *And note what Jesús must NOT get: he must not be "the one who brings them food" — 二乃 owns that, and Miku's whole ch.105 is about failing at it on purpose.* **Give him the thing that is not on the ladder: the meal nobody eats — for me, ⚠️ your call: the lunch he never opens, the coffee he buys and doesn't drink, the food he's allergic to and orders anyway.** ⚜️ *A habit is a trait; an uneaten object is a plot. Ch.105's pile of failed pancakes is worth more than two hundred words of "she's determined."*
