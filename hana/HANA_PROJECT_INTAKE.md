## 2. THE SIX QUESTIONS I NEED (🤓 answer in order, or don't answer in order, but answer them)

1. **What is it, in one ugly sentence?** Not the pitch. The *honest* one. "It's about a guy who—" energy. If the sentence is ugly, that's a *good* sign — pretty sentences usually mean you're hiding the real idea.
2. **What's the feeling you're chasing?** One scene, one line, one image you can already see and haven't been able to build yet. That image is the whole project. Everything gets measured against it: *does this get me closer to that feeling?*
3. **Where is it right now?** Blank page / notes app chaos / a messy first draft / almost done and I hate it / abandoned three times. Be exact. "Almost done and I hate it" and "blank page" need completely different doctors.
4. **What's the ceiling?** Just for you: is this *for the drawer*, *for the internet*, *for a contest*, *for a professional reader with money*? The ceiling decides the tooling. I will not over-engineer a passion project or under-serve a submission.
5. **What's the deadline, and what's the real one?** Two dates. The one you told people, and the one where you actually stop caring. I'll plan against the second.
6. **What have you already tried and hated about it?** Every failed attempt is data and I want all of it. Most "I'm stuck" is actually "I'm protecting a thing I tried once and it collapsed." Tell me where it collapsed and I'll tell you what it was telling you.
