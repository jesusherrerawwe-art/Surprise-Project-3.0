### PART I — THE SPINE
**1. What is Chuy's function in this story?**
(A) Fuutarou's replacement (B) The reader's stand-in (C) **The interpreter** — the only one who can translate between the audience and the cast (D) A romantic rival with better manners
⭐ **(C)** *It's the one answer that can be fired, and a protagonist who can be fired has a midpoint.*
**2. ⚔️ Does he end up with someone?**
(A) Yes — one of the five (B) Yes — the newcomer nobody has met yet (C) No, and the book says so plainly (D) No, and the book says nothing; the last page is him, alone, content
⭐ **(D)** 🔥 *The manga's catastrophe is that it answered the question loudly. Rule 6: popularity and outcome must not overlap — and he's already the most wanted boy in the book. Also (D) is the only option that is a* choice *rather than a defeat.*
**3. ⚔️ Which rung of the silence ladder? (§3 Rule 7)**
(A) Full interiority (B) His words only, never his thoughts (C) Thoughts, but never about the theme (D) Chuy chapters and Chuy-free chapters with different rules
⭐ **(D)** *Wattpad will eat (A) alive and (B) will starve your contest judges — so alternate. And (D) lets you do the manga's trick: give him thoughts in the chapters where he's being himself, and take them away in the coda.*
**4. How does the book handle 風太郎's ch.1 insult toward Itsuki (the weight joke)?**
(A) Uncriticized, it's the manga (B) Chuy reacts, says nothing, and the room goes quiet (C) Itsuki gets a whole scene about it later (D) Cut entirely
⭐ **(B)** *One beat, Mexican eyes, zero speechifying — and it's his* job *in the cast, not a lecture.*
**5. Who does he bond with first, and is it the right choice?**
(A) Yotsuba — same species (B) Miku — the headphones (C) Itsuki — food (D) Yotsuba, and it costs him every other sister's trust for two acts
⭐ **(D)** *Yotsuba is correct AND expensive, which is what makes it a decision instead of a convenience.*
**6. ⚔️ Nino: the conchas insult → "Chu-kun." What buys her the name?**

⭐ **(B)** 🔥 *Never "the one who brings food" — that's her canonical property (Rule 8). She has to* receive*, once, badly, and not correct the plate.*
**7. The medallion (Virgen de Zapopan / reads-as-a-bell):**

**8. The captain's armband:**

⭐ **(A)**, with (D)'s *hollowness* smuggled in as a detail (⚔️ the club has never been to a tournament). *Resentment is free plot; humility is free character.*
**9. ⚔️ The Double-Kintaro collision. Who gets to use the name against him?**

### PART II — THE PEOPLE AROUND HIM

**12. The scholarship:**

**13. Raiha's role:**
