**Jesús:** ⚔️ **a boy who pays rent on every room he is in, in the only currency that doesn't require him to say why he wants to stay.** ✅

**Three sentences of proof, all from Kevin's own text:** (1) he offers to pay 風太郎 **double** to be allowed in a room; (2) 「he would choose a perfect photograph over an afternoon of worksheets… **taking pictures of small things before they can leave**」; (3) the salsa is 「**rationed like treasure**」 — *a kindness metered out because it is finite, which is a man who has never once believed a good thing will keep coming.*

## THE MECHANISM (and it is the reason he isn't a Mary-Sue)
**His two languages are food and scent. Both are defined by disappearing.** ⚔️ **He is constitutionally incapable of a durable declaration — which is why he can *read* everyone and cannot *speak* to anyone, why his lies are loud and his truths are casseroles.** 🔒 *It also satisfies §3 Rule 7 by accident: his interiority can stay thin, because a boy built this way would rather hand you a plate than tell you anything.*
**⇒ Structural law for the book: he may never win an argument with words. He wins with an action that arrives late, in public, and is misinterpreted at least once.**

## THE COST (I will not pretend there isn't one)
⚠️ **The design is too *answered*.** Every flaw he has was built to fix a hole in a franchise I have read 60,000 words about. **A character who solves the source text perfectly reads as a solution, not a person.** 🤓 *Handle: give him one flaw with no payoff — something that costs him and fixes nothing, is never explained, and never comes back.* **A defect without a thesis is the fastest way to make a perfect character smell like a human.** 😜 *My pick, free: he is bad at saying goodbye. **I wrote "four countries" up there and Kevin was right to stop me: the dossier supports exactly ONE farewell, and he was AT it — the neighborhood despedida, all night, music and football in the street.** ☠️ **So the flaw, corrected and actually better: he has never been the one who leaves. Every departure in his life was a party thrown for him.** ⇒ *which is precisely why he'll be catastrophic at the scholarship choice:* **he has no practice being the one who goes, only the one who is seen off.** *The invented line is retracted here, in-file, and the commit history keeps the mistake — house law.*) That trait is load-bearing for nothing. It will be the truest page in your book.*

## READINESS
**He is ready to be loved in Houston.** ⭐ Not because he is charming — because he is *legible in one sentence*, and the readers who are not the bride, not the popular one, not the genius, will find him in a hallway and recognize themselves. ♥️💚🩷💜💛
