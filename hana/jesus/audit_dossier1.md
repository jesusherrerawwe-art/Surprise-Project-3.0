## B. THE COLLISIONS — four real problems, each with its handle

### B1. ⚠️ **THE MEDALLION MISMATCH (image vs. dossier) — fix before anyone sees the picture next to the text**
**The dossier: 「a small medallion of the *Virgen de Zapopan* that Sakura bought him… the last gift. He is not especially devout, but he has never taken it off.」**
**The image: a silver chain with a pendant that reads as a *small bell*.**
🔥 **I read it as a bell for a full minute before he told me otherwise — and I am the person who has written 3,000 words about 誓いの鐘.** **Every reader who has read this encyclopedia will do the same, faster.** ☠️ *Two consequences: (a) the ambiguity will spawn a fan theory that he *is* the bell-boy — which is a fine theory to have and a terrible one to have accidentally; (b) a Virgen medal is unreadable to a Japanese in-world audience and to ~half the English readership, so its *meaning* won't land, while a bell's will.*
**Handle, in order of preference:** **(i) Draw the Zapopan silhouette clearly** (her cloak + the mountain-less oval + no child — the Virgin of Zapopan is famously depicted *without* the infant Jesus, which is her actual iconographic tell and a *magnificent* hidden rhyme with "the one who is missing"). **(ii) Move it under the shirt** — a chain nobody sees is a vow, and Rule 13 wants an object that *cannot circulate*. **(iii) Keep the bell ambiguity deliberately** and let him be asked about it exactly once, in the last chapter, by 二乃, and let the answer be the sentence. **I would pick (ii) for Chapter 1 and (iii) for Chapter 24.**

### B2. ⚠️ **SELF-CONTRADICTION: 「a face that has never once successfully hidden what it is feeling」 vs. 「a remarkably poor liar」**
**Those are different characters.** The first is transparent (an openness); the second is *actively concealing and failing* (a tell). ⚠️ *The difference is not stylistic — it's who gets to read him.* **Transparency means everyone can; being a bad liar means one person can.**
**Handle:** keep the second, delete the first. **"He lies the way a smoker lies — the panic is the tell, and the tell is only legible to whoever is looking for it."** 🔒 Then make exactly **one** sister able to read him (§3's Rule 5 says give the cast a private order nobody can see: he can see *through her*; she can see *through him*; nobody else knows this exists). **I would pick 四葉 — because 「even Yotsuba can tell something is wrong」 is already in the dossier, and because §3's census gives her the extra if-chapter, so she has the screen time to earn it.** ⚠️ *And because in the manga, Yotsuba is the one who was seen at the bell and never said so.* **If your book lets Yotsuba (or your equivalent) be the one who reads him, you have paid off 122 chapters of the property's own debt — that is the 「at least at first」 clause Kevin set for 風太郎, executed on a different character.**

### B3. 🔥 **THE GRIEF ISOTOPY — his flaw is the sisters' flaw, and that is a danger, not a bonus**
**Antonio:** 「after Sakura's death he buries much of his grief in his work」. **The Nakano father:** 馬流/マルオ **likewise** buried himself in work and failed to see his granddaughters (「the man who could not tell his own granddaughters apart」, §3 §2.1). ⚠️ *Two families, same mechanism, both fathers.* 🔥 **Which makes 「luxury high-rise, a short walk from the Nakano penthouse」 not a housing decision but a *diagnosis*: the two households are structurally identical and the kids are the only difference.**
**The risk:** if Jesús's defining wound is *also* "self-erasure in the name of family," he is not a new character — he is 五月 with a different passport. ☠️ **That is §3 Rule 3: the Mary-Sue charge lands *before* he does anything, and the fix is consequence, not apology.**
**Handle, and it's a small one:** **give his mother's lesson a specifically *Mexican* social form that Japanese self-erasure does not have — and make it cost him something in the Nakano house where it doesn't read as virtue.** *Jesús's「bajarle dos rayitas al volumen」 problem is already in the dossier: in Guadalajara, generosity at maximum is normal; in the Nakano house it is a *symptom*.* **⇒ Write one scene where his kindness is correctly read by his father's culture as love and incorrectly read by the sisters' culture as pity — and *nobody is wrong*.** That single scene makes him irreducible.

### B4. ⚠️ **THE CAPTAIN-FOOTBALL PROBLEM — a plot hole you can close in one clause**
**「Club: Football club — captain (more on how *that* happened below)」** ⚠️ — and "below" hasn't arrived yet (that's §10). The problem is structural, not factual: **a second-year transfer student, two years of Japanese language development behind, made captain of a Japanese school football club in his first months is the one sentence in the dossier a skeptical judge will read as wish-fulfillment.** 🔒 *The property's own precedent is the opposite and better: 風太郎, a genius, finished 6th of 7 in the official character poll (§3 §2.1: 81 votes, behind 前田).*
**Handle, three options, pick one and write it in:** **(i) He is captain of the *wrong* team** — e.g. the club's manager got confused, or he was elected by the first-years, or he is "captain" of a club that is effectively eleven people and a wall; **the joke is that the prestige is fake and his feelings about it are real.** **(ii) He earned it and it is *resented*** — one senior who will never let him forget it, who appears exactly twice, both times at the worst moment. **(iii) He is *refusing* to be captain** and Chapter 10 is about that. 😜 **I'd take (ii), because §3 Rule 6's law is that popularity and outcome must never overlap: if he is the most admired new boy at Asahiyama *and* wins the story, you have made the mistake the property made.**
