### ⑦ **THE MAKEOVER'S LOAD-BEARING CHANGE: the mother's secret must COST him something, not teach him something.**
🔒 In §17 as written, the reveal gives Chuy three lines of self-hatred, and then he runs away. ⚠️ **That is grief as *information*. It is the manga's exact mistake aimed at its own reader.** 🔥 **v3: the reveal must make him DO the wrong thing, expensively.** ⚔️ **Concretely: after the box, he becomes his mother.** He starts hiding his own grade from his father, hides the scholarship, hides the running away — *for everyone's own good* — and **the book's most painful scene is one of the sisters, who has done this her whole life, screaming at him to stop.** 🔒 *Miku, in canon, is the only one who ever walks into the boys' stall alone to fix a war she isn't in (§3, ch.105). She is the correct witness.*
> ### **RULE 23 — A SECRET MUST CONVERT INTO A BEHAVIOR IN THE NEXT CHAPTER OR IT WAS PLOT, NOT CHARACTER**
> *Test: name the first act your protagonist performs after the reveal that he would not have performed before it.* ⚔️ *"He leaves" does not count — leaving is a reaction.* 🔥 *What counts: he lies kindly, he hoards, he refuses help, he cancels his own future to protect a feeling. Then the ending has to break the behavior, not the information.* ☠️ *Your manuscript currently breaks the information, which is why §17's last line — "he might have no reasons to come back" — has to be earned by an action he takes, not a mood he's in.*
