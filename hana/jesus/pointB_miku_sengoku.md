## 7. ⚠️ HOW TO BUILD THE EXAM WITHOUT BREAKING THE WORLD
**A Japanese public-school 期末試験 in 世界史 will NOT contain a Mexican Revolution unit** — 世界史B covers 近代ラテンアメリカ *briefly* at best, and a whole 単元 on Juárez is not realistic (☠️ and a Wattpad reader with one Japanese friend will say so). **Use one of three doors:**
1. 🔒 **Best: make it the teacher's own 探究/プレゼン assignment** — 「自分の興味のある歴史を一つ選べ」, which is a real modern curriculum device (探究学習) and lets 竹林 set a personal trap. **He knows she loves warlords; he dares her to love something else.**
2. **Second: it is 小論文 (essay exam)** — the one format where a foreign subject is legitimate, and the one format 風太郎 can't help anyone with, which *forces* her to him. ⚔️ **Structure: 風太郎 is useless in this chapter. That's not a flaw in your plan, that's the plot.**
3. ⚠️ **Third: make the Mexican content come from HIM, not the test** — he corrects her research in the library. *Weaker for the exam beat, stronger for the relationship.*
**My pick: (1) with (2) as the grade at stake.** 🔒 *It also re-runs the property's own engine — S1's whole first arc is 「期末試験で赤点取ったら家庭教師クビ」 — and §2's census has 五月 as the only sister with an extra if-chapter because her exam block is the largest. You are playing on the franchise's home field.*
